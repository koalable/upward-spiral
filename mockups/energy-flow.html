How to read it: each group has its own theme colour. The shade of a tile shows momentum: pale = slipping, deep = surging. The label and the little 6-week bars say the same thing, so it never relies on colour alone.

Momentum rule (proposed): this week against your average of the three weeks before. More than 20% up = surging, 5–20% up = rising, within 5% = steady, 5–20% down = dipping, more than 20% down = slipping. Early in the week it compares the same days.
